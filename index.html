What is WBTC

What you get when you buy WBTC

WBTC (Wrapped Bitcoin) is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum that is backed 1:1 by BTC held by a custodian. To buy WBTC here, you swap a token you already hold for it, and the WBTC arrives in your own wallet.

Who this is for

People who already have crypto in an Ethereum wallet and want Bitcoin price exposure they can use in Ethereum apps. This interface does not sell for cards or bank transfers, so you need ETH, USDC, USDT or another ERC-20 token first.

How the swap works

The interface asks DEX routing to search liquidity pools and returns a quote. You review it and sign from your wallet; the swap settles on-chain.

WBTC vs BTC

WBTC vs native BTC

WBTC tracks the price of Bitcoin, but it is a different asset on a different network. The differences matter most for custody and redemption.

The trade-off

Holding WBTC adds custodian and smart contract risk that native BTC does not have. In return you get a token that Ethereum applications can handle.

How to buy

How to buy WBTC

Before you start

You need an Ethereum wallet that already holds ETH, USDC, USDT or another ERC-20 token. Keep some ETH for gas even if you plan to sell a stablecoin.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and choose the token you want to sell, with WBTC as the token to buy.
  2. Enter an amount and review the quote, route, price impact and estimated gas.
  3. If you sell an ERC-20 token such as USDC or USDT, approve it once for this wallet. ETH needs no approval.
  4. Sign the swap in your wallet and wait for it to confirm on Ethereum.
  5. Check that the WBTC balance appears in your wallet; add the token contract manually if your wallet does not show it.
Fees & gas

What buying WBTC costs

Gas

Gas is paid in ETH. The approval, if needed, and the swap are separate Ethereum transactions, each with its own gas cost. See how gas works.

Trading cost

The quote already reflects pool or aggregator fees and price impact. Your slippage tolerance sets how far the final price may move before the swap reverts.

Keep ETH in the wallet

If you sell USDC or USDT, you still need native ETH to pay gas. Without it you cannot approve or sign the swap.

Security

Staying safe when you buy WBTC

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface routes and quotes; it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify the WBTC contract address; fake and lookalike tokens exist.
  • Approve only what you need, and revoke old ERC-20 allowances you no longer use.
  • Check the quote, price impact and slippage before confirming.
  • WBTC depends on a custodian and on smart contracts, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Buy WBTC FAQ

Can I buy WBTC with a card or bank transfer here?

No. This interface only swaps crypto you already hold. You need ETH, USDC, USDT or another ERC-20 token in an Ethereum wallet before you can buy WBTC.

Which tokens can I use to buy WBTC?

You can sell ETH, USDC, USDT or other ERC-20 tokens that DEX routing can find liquidity for. The quote shows the route and expected WBTC before you sign.

Is WBTC the same as Bitcoin?

No. WBTC is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum backed 1:1 by BTC held by a custodian. It follows Bitcoin's price but depends on that custodian and on smart contracts.

Why do I need to approve a token before swapping?

ERC-20 tokens such as USDC and USDT need a one-time allowance so the router can move them for you. This is a separate transaction that costs gas. Selling ETH needs no approval.

What do I pay gas in when I buy WBTC?

Gas on Ethereum is always paid in ETH, even when you sell a stablecoin. Keep some ETH in the wallet or you will not be able to approve or sign the swap.

Can I turn WBTC back into BTC?

WBTC is redeemed for BTC through the custodian's own process, which this interface does not handle. You can also swap WBTC back to ETH or a stablecoin here at the quoted market price.
